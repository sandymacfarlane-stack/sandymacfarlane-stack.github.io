Personal IS

Personal IS is a private assistant that one person runs for their own household administration. It keeps a register of tasks and work, prepares a short daily briefing, and backs up its own records. It is not offered to anyone else and has one user: its owner.

What it uses from Google

  • Google Drive, only the files Personal IS creates. It writes its own backup files to a folder in its owner's Drive and reads them back to check them. It cannot see or change any other file.
  • Gmail, read-only, only if its owner grants it separately. If granted, it reads its owner's mail from the last 90 days through a protected service that cannot send, forward or delete mail.
