Privacy policy

Personal IS is run by its owner for their own use. It has no other users. This page explains what it does with Google user data.

Google data it accesses

  • Google Drive (the drive.file permission). Only files that Personal IS itself creates: its backup files, in a folder in its owner's Drive. It cannot see or change any other file.
  • Gmail (the gmail.readonly permission), only if its owner grants it. Messages from the last 90 days, read through a protected service that cannot send, forward, delete or move mail to Trash.

How the data is used

Only to provide Personal IS's own features to its owner: keeping backups of its records, and, if Gmail is granted, reading recent mail so that it can prepare summaries and draft suggestions for its owner to review. Nothing is sent from the owner's mailbox.

Service providers

Personal IS uses these services to do its work for its owner:

  • Google Cloud runs Personal IS and stores its records, in the owner's own project in the London region.
  • Anthropic's Claude API may receive text, including text from the owner's mail if Gmail is granted, to prepare summaries and draft suggestions for the owner. Anthropic may process it outside the UK.
  • Notion, in the owner's own workspace, shows the owner the daily briefing, tasks and decisions that Personal IS prepares.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising, and is not shared with anyone except the service providers above, which process it only to provide these features to the owner. Personal IS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Backups are kept in the owner's own Google Drive. Personal IS's records are kept in the owner's own Google Cloud project in the London region, and what it shows the owner is kept in the owner's own Notion workspace. They are kept until the owner deletes them.

Withdrawing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google Account, under third-party connections.

Changes

Any change to this policy is published on this page with a new date.
